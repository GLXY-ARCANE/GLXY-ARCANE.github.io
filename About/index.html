About Me

Just more detailed!

Hey, I’m GLXY_ARCANE!

I’m an enthusiastic content creator with a deep passion for YouTube, Coding, and storytelling. I strive to craft videos that not only entertain but truly engage my audience. My content is all about bringing a bit of fun and excitement, whether I'm gaming or just hanging out.

In addition to making videos, I’ve also streamed on Kick, Twitch, and YouTube. While I no longer stream on Kick, I’ve considered returning to Twitch in the future, and I’m always looking for new ways to connect with my audience through live streams.

One of my greatest passions is Minecraft—I’ve spent countless hours creating incredible builds like a massive Charmander statue, a mega castle base, and a whole city. Beyond Minecraft, I’ve played several Call of Duty games including MW2 (2022), MW3 (2023), Black Ops 6, and Infinite Warfare Zombies. In fact, I even managed to unlock the Director’s Cut and complete nearly all the easter eggs in the game! I love diving deep into these games, and I'm always up for new challenges.

I’m constantly working on improving my skills, whether it’s learning new coding techniques or finding ways to step up my content. One of my big goals is to reach 1,000 subscribers by my birthday (February 8th), and one day, I hope to hit 1 million subs. With your support, I know I can make it!

Goals:
