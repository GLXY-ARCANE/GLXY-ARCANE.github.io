Gas play history

Gas playing is loved by many but hated by even more. With every update and new event, the landscape of gas plays keeps evolving. But where did it all begin? How did gas plays become such a defining part of the game?

Today, we’re diving into the history of gas plays—exploring how they started, how they’ve changed over time, and what’s shaped them into what they are now. This is part one of a two-part series. If you’re excited to see where this journey goes, make sure to subscribe so you don’t miss part two!

Chapter I: BLACKOUT

When people think of gas plays, they usually associate them with Warzone. But Call of Duty’s history with battle royales goes back further—to Blackout, the mode introduced in Call of Duty: Black Ops 4. Blackout replaced the traditional campaign in BO4. And thanks to TurtleSloth, we’ve uncovered what we believe is the very first documented zero-kill gas play. Let’s roll the clip:

clip of OJA_95 gas play

Blackout launched on October 12, 2018, and this clip was posted just 10 days later, on October 22. It marked the earliest known instance of a gas play. But that wasn’t the only example. The very next day, Widdz uploaded a video titled "INSANE 0 KILL WIN in Blackout Solo!" was posted by a YouTube named Widdz Let’s check it out:

clip of Widdz gas play

During this time, gas plays were fairly straightforward. The strategy relied heavily on stocking up on heals and positioning just outside the safe zone. The gas in Blackout inflicted 25 damage per tick, so running out of heals was a guaranteed loss. It was a simple yet effective method for securing a win without firing a single shot.

One more notable example comes from a player named It’s Mr. Lone Wolf, who also executed a gas play and shared his thoughts in the video comments. Let’s take a look at the clip first:

clip of It's Mr. Lone Wolf gas play

Mr. Lone Wolf commented his thoughts on this in his comments, he said, and please not this summarized:

"This adds an interesting dynamic to the game because it introduces a strategy that shooting skill alone can't counter. If the collapse favors certain locations, players need to consider tactics beyond traditional gunfights. For example, if you're at a disadvantage—low health, no ammo—you can use perks like Consumer to outlast opponents in a collapse. While rare, it’s not overpowered or cheap; it’s simply another way to win."

he also shared a personal story about how this strategy was used against him, but if you’d like to read the full story or listen to it as he talks about it in the video, you can find the link in the description below.

And with that, we wrap up the Blackout segment. While this era didn’t offer a lot of variety in gas play strategies, it proved one thing: you don’t need to kill to win in battle royale. Blackout laid the foundation for what would later evolve into a key part of Warzone’s meta.

Chapter II: The Rise of Gas plays..

Warzone 1 is what brough Gas playing to the main stage. with the launch of Warzone on March 10, 2020—right smack in the middle of the COVID-19 lockdowns (a time none of us will likely forget anytime soon)—it didn’t take long for players to start experimenting with creative strategies. On the very same day Warzone launched, a player by the name of VigilAndy posted a groundbreaking video titled, "WARZONE Solo 0 Kills/Damage Win!!!".

Cue clip of VigilAndy gas play

While VigilAndy didn’t rely on stims for his win, he did use a helicopter which is very common to do while gas playing now. Floating in a helicopter above the chaos and letting the gas eliminate every other player—it was smart and downright kinda funny too. But the story didn’t stop there. Just 15 days later, another was uploaded it and it. blew. up.

Enter Vikkstar123

On March 25th, Vikkstar123 released his gas play video, which skyrocketed to 1.9 million views in a matter of months. This was the video that really opened the floodgates, introducing gas plays to a global audience. It wasn’t just a win; it was a masterclass in absurd, tactical gameplay. Watching him and his team survive purely with stims, gas masks, and precise timing was nothing short of mesmerizing. The strategy was so effective—and so infuriating to opponents—that is sparked something in more people

One of the best things from the video came not during the gameplay itself, but in the comments section. The second top comment, written by a user named JasonMighty, perfectly summed up the community’s reaction:

"Imagine working so hard with 7+ kills each to then lose to a team of meme artists with 0 kills each using stims and masks."

This comment wasn’t just funny; it encapsulated the brilliance and audacity of gas plays. They defied conventional Warzone logic, turning the idea of victory into something as much about creativity as skill. And yet, as groundbreaking as these early videos were, they were just the tip of the iceberg.

After Vikkstar’s viral moment, gas plays didn’t dominate the Warzone meta overnight. In fact, there was a bit of a lull. Months went by without much notable innovation—or at least, without any widely shared footage. That is, until May 25th, when another pivotal moment in gas play history unfolded...

Chapter IV: MODERN TWO

With the launch of Warzone 2.0 came one very specific item that helped so... so much... Backpacks.

With a Large Backpack, you could suddenly carry up to 20 Stims at once—22 if you factored in a Munitions Box. This wasn't just a minor improvement; it was a revolution. No longer was a Stim run a desperate scramble; it was a calculated resource-gathering mission.

The addition of Medical Cabinets in POIs made farming Stims and Self-Revives incredibly easy, and the Revive Pistol provided even more emergency self-revives. All of this combined to turn the gas from a deadly barrier into a safe zone without any other players.

Warzone Three

Warzone 3 brought a major shift in gas playing, because when it was first released, there wasn't any gas tick, meaning you could survive an entire game in the gas... Oh hey someone did that! But there were also changes in the inventory system that did effect some things..

Jamcowl Interview Point:
If you could change one thing about the game to say make gas plays and stuff easier, what would it be?

As I said before, in warzone 2 you could have 22 stims at once, now you could only have 12.. 14 if you count a Muni box. Now you may think that this would make it generally hard, but not really. Thanks to strats like having a stim stash, you only needed to get to said stash. Once you were there then your good to go. Not to mention that along with Warzone 3 came very common PDS's, aka Portable Decontamination Stations, that would make a safe bubble around you letting you live for even longer...

Sadly, after a while, the gas tick got patched though. Before the patch, it was a solid 10 damage per second.. after the patch, once you were 50 seconds in the gas, it would do an extra 5+ damage for every 10 seconds in the gas. Meaning, by the 3 minute mark, there was what we called the Stim wall. This was the point in which you were taking more damage compared to how much you were healing back up from the stims.

Chapter V: WARZONE TODAY

Ah yes, Black ops 6 Warzone, which I will be calling Warzone 4, its where we are at today! And what's awesome is that in Warzone 4, a few things got buffed! The stim animation is faster, and we got a cool vest to let us have even more stims.. but that's mostly it. Over the first few seasons, there wasn't anything all that good. Its when we had Area 99, and honestly, that map wasn't the greatest and I personally don't have any good rememberable moments on it because it sucked...

Over time though, the developers were stating that gas playing was a cheese strat, that it wasn't a fair play style, and they felt that they had to nerf it now.. And so, in season 2, we got the nerf of no more Munition boxes if you are more then ~40 meters out from the zone. Which hey that's not a big deal..? Right..? Nah not really. But over the span of bo6, the rest of the changes really mess with things. We got the Munitions vest which let us have 3 stims instead of 2, and then it got removed, with only a few ways to get it. And now the only way to get it is with the scanner cards. We also had Medicine cabinets, until season 5 where they are now completely removed
